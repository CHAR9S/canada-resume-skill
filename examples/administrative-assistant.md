# Worked example: office support to administrative assistant

All people, organizations, experience, and job requirements below are fictional. This is an authored demonstration of the workflow, not a real application or measured benchmark.

## Input: target posting

Administrative Assistant, Toronto. Required: scheduling, written customer communication, Excel recordkeeping, and document organization. Preferred: CRM experience. Submit an English resume; no special page or file requirement supplied.

## Input: candidate notes

Alex Morgan, Toronto, Ontario; alex.morgan@example.com.

- Office Assistant, Cedar Example Services, Toronto, June 2023–September 2025.
- Booked meetings in Outlook for a six-person team.
- Answered customer email about appointment times and service documents.
- Updated the weekly Excel request tracker; used filters and basic formulas, not pivot tables.
- Reorganized shared folders using consistent file names; colleagues could find documents by client and month. No time-saving measurement.
- Trained two new colleagues on the request tracker.
- Office Administration Diploma, Example College, Toronto, completed 2023.
- No CRM experience stated. No additional languages or work authorization details supplied.

## Diagnosis

The notes contain direct evidence for the four required areas. Place scheduling, customer communication, and Excel work early. Retain the qualitative document-organization outcome; do not invent a percentage. CRM is preferred and not evidenced, so it should not be added as a skill.
