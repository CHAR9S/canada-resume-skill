# Sources and editorial decisions

Reviewed: 2026-10-31. This is an independent project by CHAR9S, not an official product of the organizations below. The rules combine source-informed guidance with explicitly described project design choices.

## Foundational handouts

A user-supplied, eight-page scan of **Centre for Skills Development** resume handouts informed the workflow. Publication date is unknown. The organization's printed web address is [centreforskills.ca](https://www.centreforskills.ca/); this is an organization link, not a verified public URL for the handouts.

| Scan pages | Topic | Use in this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 6 | Resume headings | Recognizable, relevant sections |
| 2 | Resume summary | Put strongest relevant qualifications early |
| 3–4 | Action verbs | Choose verbs matching actual work |
| 5 | Resume content and appearance | Tailoring, recent relevance, concise presentation |
| 7–8 | Accomplishment statements | Connect context, action, and supported outcomes |

The scan and its verb lists, branded artwork, and worked examples are **not redistributed**. The repository's instructions, diagrams, and fictional examples are newly written. The MIT licence covers this project's original contributions, not the source handouts or linked sites.

## Public cross-checks

- [WorkBC: Resume and Cover Letter](https://www.workbc.ca/get-support/job-application-tips/resume-and-cover-letter): supports relevance-led organization, job-specific language, ordinary one-to-two-page guidance, and providing references when requested. Full page reviewed.
- [Government of Canada Job Bank: How to write a good resume](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume): indexed guidance supports honesty and omitting photos in ordinary Canadian resumes. Search excerpts reviewed; direct page retrieval timed out during preparation. No broader claims rely on unread portions.
- [Alberta alis: Avoid Making Common Mistakes on Your Resumé](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/avoid-making-common-mistakes-on-your-resume/): indexed guidance says not to include a SIN. Search excerpt reviewed.

## What changed from the handouts

- A fixed four-to-six-statement summary becomes optional and evidence-led.
- The Office 2010 example becomes “name tools actually used”; no obsolete version is prescribed.
- Reviewing 10–20 advertisements becomes an optional role-exploration exercise. One posting is sufficient for a targeted application.
- A 10–15-year history guideline becomes a relevance decision, not automatic deletion.
- Quantified examples become a process for asking about real results. No example metric may be transferred to an applicant.
- A fixed recruiter reading time is not repeated as a universal fact.

Evidence tables, the three evidence statuses, privacy-conscious defaults, the export checklist, and refusal to assign an ATS score are project design choices. They are not claimed as a government standard or a hiring-system certification.
