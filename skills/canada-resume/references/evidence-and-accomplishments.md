# Evidence and accomplishment writing

## Requirement map

Use a compact working table. For a short edit this can remain internal; for full tailoring show the useful rows.

| Job requirement | Candidate evidence | Status | Editing decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Excel reporting | Candidate says they updated a weekly Excel tracker | Supported | Name Excel and the reporting task |
| Pivot tables | No detail supplied | Needs clarification | Ask; do not add proficiency |
| CRM experience preferred | No CRM work mentioned | Not evidenced | Leave out; flag as a preference |

Reference the supplied source, e.g. “Experience, role 2, bullet 1” or “follow-up answer.” Do not label absent evidence a personal deficiency. Separate employer requirements from your suggestions.

## Interview for useful evidence

Ask about the job-relevant work: what problem existed, what the person actually did, what changed, and what they can reasonably substantiate. Useful detail includes frequency, volume, scope, tools, turnaround time, quality, service outcomes, recognition, or collaboration. A person does not need financial metrics to have valuable experience.

If a number is offered, clarify its unit, timeframe, baseline, ownership, and whether it is approximate where those affect the claim. Do not convert “handled 20 requests daily” into “improved productivity by 20%.” Preserve approximate language and avoid false precision.

## Original examples (fictional)

**Raw:** “Helped with inventory.”

**Confirmed detail:** Checked deliveries against packing slips, logged discrepancies in Excel, and flagged them to the supervisor. No measured outcome supplied.

**Grounded rewrite:** “Checked deliveries against packing slips, recorded discrepancies in Excel, and flagged mismatches to the supervisor.”

**Do not add:** “Reduced inventory errors by 35%.” The source contains no such measurement.

**Raw:** “Helped new staff.”

**Confirmed detail:** Demonstrated the closing checklist to three new colleagues over the summer. No supervisory responsibility.

**Grounded rewrite:** “Trained three new colleagues on the closing checklist during the summer.”

**Do not add:** “Managed a team of three.” Training is not people management.

**Raw:** “Made reports faster.”

**Confirmed detail:** A weekly report previously took about 90 minutes; after the candidate reorganized an Excel template it took about 60 minutes. Both are user estimates.

**Grounded rewrite:** “Reorganized the Excel reporting template, reducing weekly preparation time from approximately 90 to 60 minutes.”

**Decision:** Use the transparent before/after values; a percentage is unnecessary. Never present an estimate as an audited measurement.

## Verbs should describe the actual contribution

Choose the most accurate verb, not the grandest: reconciled for resolving records, coordinated for organizing work across people, assisted for support, maintained for ongoing upkeep, analysed for examination, designed for creating a solution, trained for instruction. “Led,” “owned,” and “transformed” require corresponding responsibility or evidence. Match tense to whether the activity is ongoing or completed.

Keep evidence notes outside the resume. The final bullet should read naturally, not as a labelled Situation/Action/Result worksheet. Preserve meaning when shortening.
