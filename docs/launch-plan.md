# Initial release and learning plan

## Positioning

An evidence-led Canadian resume skill: target a real posting, improve the way experience is expressed, and keep every claim grounded. The first demonstration should be the worked example, not a promise about interview rates.

## First month

1. Publish 0.1.0 with English/Chinese documentation, sources, and a reproducible example.
2. Invite people to try one narrow task and report which edits they kept or rejected. Share only where community rules allow; do not mass-message or solicit artificial stars.
3. Triage issues into factual guidance, model behavior, installation friction, and missing contexts.
4. Revise the most common demonstrated problem, rerun affected cases, and record the change.

## Signals worth recording

| Signal | What it helps assess | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stars and followers | Interest and discoverability | Not evidence of installation or useful output |
| Repository views/clones, where available | Traffic and intent | Not unique active users; platform retention is limited |
| Voluntary usage reports | Whether people complete a task and keep edits | Self-selected sample |
| Recurring questions/issues | Friction and missing coverage | Requires interpretation, not just counting |
| Contributions | Whether others find it worth improving | Small early counts are inconclusive |

Record a baseline after publication and compare weekly manually. No telemetry or automated reporting is added. Do not collect applicant resumes to measure adoption.

## Next-release candidates

Only add features supported by feedback: additional fictional career-change examples, independently reviewed French output, and repeatable cross-client behavioral runs. A plugin package or document exporter can follow if installation or export friction justifies it.
