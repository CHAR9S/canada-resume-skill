# Community expectations

Discuss people's work respectfully. Welcome applicants across backgrounds, career stages, languages, and countries of experience. Critique a resume choice or a rule, not a person's identity or worth.

Do not post harassment, discriminatory content, spam, private personal data, or confidential employer information. Use fictional or carefully redacted examples.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or limit participation to protect the community. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive or private content; do not repeat it in another public thread. For ordinary project disputes, describe the specific behavior and requested correction without personal attacks.
